The Investi-cattor
==================

looking into irrelevant things like murders, but refuse to even consider the case
of the missing tuna. I mean, what's more serious than a tuna heist?! My human won't
lift a finger to help - and they dare to call cats lazy! - so I'm going to have to
investigate it myself.

I shall become.... The Investicattor.
