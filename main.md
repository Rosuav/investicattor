## Part One: The Shocking Discovery

Discovery: Tuna is missing. Lots of it. Like, enough to make a real meal out of. Maybe even enough for the humans to care.

Human investigator declines to dig into it. No proof that a crime has even happened. Tells cat "if you think it's so important, YOU investigate".

Cat takes him up on it. Tuna is just that important.

## Part Two: The Crime Scene

A family home. A cat, practically starving to death, because this morning's meal of tuna was not provided. (No, the dry food does not count. I cannot survive on that stuff. Though I will eat it. Of course.) The delivery of tuna to the house was interrupted; it MUST be grand theft!

Places to investigate:

* Food bowl
* Kitchen cupboard
* Adult human (busy)
* Smaller human (wants to pick you up)
* Vase on mantlepiece
* Refrigerator

As this is functionally the tutorial, you will be able to click on every clickable; however there will be a decrementing counter of how many more you have time to explore. Use clock symbols to hint that it's not "how much left in the scene".

Once investigation here is complete, other locations open up. They can be visited in any order.
